1. Overview

NeuroLite is a daily brain training application designed to support regular mental activity through logic-based tasks. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting user data.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

NeuroLite does not require account registration and does not request personal information such as name, phone number, or address. Purchases are tied to the Apple ID or Google account that pays for them. We do not receive payment card details.

2.2 Usage and Diagnostic Data

  • App interactions (screens viewed, puzzles completed, streak information)
  • Technical diagnostics (crash reports, device type, operating system version)

2.3 Identifiers

The app may collect device identifiers and advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA) for analytics, diagnostics, advertising, and subscription management purposes. These identifiers are not used to personally identify users.

3. Advertising

NeuroLite may display advertisements in current or future versions of the app. Advertisements are provided by third-party ad networks. We do not control the content of third-party advertisements and are not responsible for their accuracy, legality, or quality.

4. Subscriptions and Purchases

NeuroLite offers an optional auto-renewable subscription, NeuroLite Plus. Plus includes practice tasks, past unsolved days, and full statistics. The daily task stays free.

There are two plans:

  • Monthly: renews every month
  • Yearly: renews every year

The price is the amount shown by Google Play or the App Store for your country and account at the moment of purchase. It is not the same in every country. The app loads this price from the store and does not set a single worldwide price.

Each plan includes a 7-day free trial when the store still offers it for your account. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the subscription continues as the paid plan you chose and the store charges that price. If this account has already used a NeuroLite Plus trial, the store charges the plan price immediately.

The subscription renews automatically for the same period, one month or one year, until you cancel. The store may charge the account up to 24 hours before the current period ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or the current paid period. After cancellation, Plus remains available until the end of the period already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Manage or cancel:

  • Google Play subscriptions
  • Apple subscriptions

Payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not collect or store payment card details. Purchases on Apple devices are also covered by the Licensed Application End User License Agreement. Purchases on Android are covered by the Google Play Terms of Service.

Download NeuroLite:
